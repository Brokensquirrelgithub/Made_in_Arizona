# Car Otaku wrap illustrations

Eight original, transparent anime inspired decals are packed into the runtime
`OtakuWraps.png` atlas by `python Tools/build-otaku-wrap-atlas.py`. The atlas is a
four column, two row grid; each cell is 512 pixels square. The game cycles the
eight cells across Car Otaku vehicles, using different art on each side.

The art prompts were for bold, cel shaded Japanese car vinyl illustrations with
transparent backgrounds and no existing franchise characters or logos:

1. Grimy turquoise mechanic heroine giving a thumbs up with racing accents.
2. Magenta desert racer heroine with stars and checkered flag accents.
3. Scarlet mecha pilot handing out fast food from a tiny service robot.
4. Magical girl changing a flat tire with a moon shaped wrench wand.
5. Shonen delivery hero powering up to deliver one cup of noodles.
6. Space bounty hunter tracking overdue fuel receipts at a desert gas pump.
7. Fantasy knight serving a parking ticket to a giant sand worm.
8. Idol mechanic singing into a torque wrench atop a stack of tires.

All eight are playful genre parodies; they use original character designs.
